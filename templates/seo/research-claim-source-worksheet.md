# Research claim and source worksheet

Use one completed copy per research page before making a result, method, or reproducibility claim more specific. Record `unknown` when a source artifact does not establish the field. Do not infer a missing sample, date, model version, statistical test, dataset, or availability status from filenames, commit history, or related articles.

## Release decision

- [ ] Visible question, method, result, and limitations agree with the source artifact.
- [ ] Numeric claims were recalculated or marked `needs owner review`.
- [ ] Citation text, canonical URL, and PDF link have been checked.
- [ ] Data/code availability is accurate and does not imply reproducibility.
- [ ] A qualified owner approved the method, limitations, conflicts, and AI-use disclosure.
