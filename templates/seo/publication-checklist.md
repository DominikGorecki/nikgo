# Publication checklist

- [ ] Intent, audience, content ID, canonical permalink, taxonomy, and relationships approved.
- [ ] Title/description are unique; opening answers the page's real question.
- [ ] Author, dates, sources, claims, quotations, links, and evidence/inference/scenario boundaries reviewed.
- [ ] Required method, limitation, health, conflict, citation, and AI-use disclosures are visible.
- [ ] Image provenance, dimensions, alt, caption/credit, and performance budget are verified.
- [ ] Visible content, metadata, schema, sitemap, hub, and related links agree.
- [ ] Locked build, validators, mobile/keyboard/accessibility checks, and preview review pass.
- [ ] Post-publish sitemap/index and representative live-page checks are recorded.
