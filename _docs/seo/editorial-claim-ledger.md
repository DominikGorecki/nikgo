# Editorial claim ledger

Use this ledger before strengthening, summarizing, or refreshing a factual claim. It distinguishes sourced observation from author inference, analogy, scenario, and high-stakes content. Record `unknown` rather than inferring an unavailable source date, methodology, reviewer, or qualification.

| Canonical URL | Claim or extract | Claim type | Supporting source | Source date | Qualification or boundary | Review status | Reviewer / review date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | Observed fact / author inference / analogy / scenario / health information | | | | | Unknown / needs review / approved | |

## Review rules

- Keep the source adjacent to non-obvious factual claims in published content.
- Do not convert a hypothesis, thought experiment, or analogy into an observed fact in a summary.
- For medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes content, record the appropriate expert review or leave the item as `needs review`.
- Verify time-sensitive claims as of the visible modified date; do not use this ledger as proof that verification happened.
